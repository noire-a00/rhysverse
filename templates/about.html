About Rhys Verse

Your Literary Universe

What is RhysVerse?

RhysVerse is a book discovery platform built with love for literature. Find, explore, and preview millions of books from around the world all in one place.

🔍

How it Works

RhysVerse is powered by the Google Books API, giving you access to millions of books across every genre imaginable. Search by title, author, or browse by category.

✨

Why RhysVerse?

Built for book lovers who want a beautiful, simple, and fast way to discover their next favorite read. No clutter, no noise just books.

Why RHYS VERSE?

You may wonder where the name RHYS VERSE came from.

The answer is simple, yet rather personal.

Rhys is a name that means something to me. It belongs to someone whose presence, in one way or another, became a small part of my story.

Perhaps he will never know how much this name means to me. Perhaps it was never meant to become anything more than a name.

So I decided to let it live somewhere else.

Here.

RHYS VERSE is my little universe of books, stories, thoughts, and things I love. And hidden quietly within its name is a person I once found worth remembering.

Not every feeling needs to become a relationship.
Some things are beautiful simply because they existed.

And perhaps this is my way of keeping a beautiful name alive, without asking it to become anything more.

About Me

Noire

@noirelysium_

A quiet mind, surrounded by stories.

I'm Noire,everyone call me Lisa in real life, I'm a university student and an aspiring librarian who has always found something comforting in books.

I have a particular fondness for stories that explore the human mind, loneliness, identity, and the strange ways people try to understand themselves. Some of the works that have stayed with me are No Longer Human by Osamu Dazai, The Metamorphosis by Franz Kafka, and White Nights by Fyodor Dostoevsky.

I don't think every book needs to give us an answer. Sometimes, a book is meaningful simply because it makes us ask better questions.

Beyond reading, I'm interested in learning languages, exploring technology, and discovering new perspectives through the things I encounter. I enjoy learning quietly, at my own pace, and collecting little pieces of knowledge along the way.

One day, I hope to become a librarian.
Not simply someone who takes care of books, but someone who helps people find the right story, the right piece of knowledge, or perhaps even a small place where they feel understood.

This website is my little library on the internet. A place for the books I've read, the stories I'm discovering, and the thoughts I want to keep.

Somewhere between the pages, I'm still figuring myself out.
